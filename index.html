Two rooms · One welcome

A working pub on the edge
of Hemel Old Town.

Not a gastropub. Not a chain. The Old Bell Gate is the kind of two-room local that still keeps a pool table in the public bar and a quiet pint in the saloon — a small Hertfordshire pub on the outskirts of Hemel Hempstead's historic old town, run for the people who actually live around it.

№ I

The Public Bar

Sports on. Cue chalked. Music up.

Where the regulars sit. Multiple TVs for the football, rugby and racing on Sky and TNT. Pool table and dartboard kept ready. Live music most Saturdays and alternate Thursdays — the room that finds its volume after seven.

  • Pool table Free play
  • Dartboard Bring your own arrows
  • Sky Sports + TNT All weekend fixtures
  • Live music Sat & alt. Thursday

№ II

The Saloon Bar

Quiet pint. Slow conversation.

A separate snug for when you'd rather not hear the match. Smaller, calmer, the room that stays measured. Bring the dog. Settle in. Ten covers across the room and a partly-covered patio garden out the back when the weather agrees.

  • Snug seating About 10 covers
  • Patio beer garden Partly covered
  • Dog friendly Always welcome
  • Wi-Fi Quiet zone

What's usually on

A week at the Bell Gate.

Hours are simple — noon until half past midnight, every day. The events list below is a guide to the regular rhythm of the place; for the actual line-up this week, give us a ring or check Facebook before you set off.

Most Saturdays

Live music in the public bar

A regular fixture — local acts, no cover charge. Volume goes up after seven.

Alternate Thursdays

Midweek music nights

Lower-key than the Saturday slot. Worth a ring ahead to see if it's on this week.

Every weekend

Sky & TNT Sports

Football, rugby, racing — multiple screens, both subscriptions, full match-day pace.

Anytime

Pool, darts, a pint of cask

Pool free to play. Tring Brewery cask ale when it's on the line. Snacks behind the bar — rolls, pizza, pies.
